# Cache Eviction Policies: LRU, LFU & FIFO

> [!TIP]
> **Interactive Diagram**: Open [diagram.html](diagram.html) in your browser to explore the interactive animated data flow with theme toggles and SVG/image export.

- **LRU (Least Recently Used)**: Discards items not accessed for the longest time.
- **LFU (Least Frequently Used)**: Discards items with lowest access frequency count.
- **FIFO (First In First Out)**: Evicts oldest items regardless of access patterns.
